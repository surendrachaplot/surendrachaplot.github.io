Sea Bass with Roast Potatoes, Tomatoes, and Olives

35 min · Serves 2

This goes full Mediterranean without becoming smug about it. Sweet tomatoes collapse into the tray, olives bring salt, and the sea bass stays clean and quick.

Ingredients

  • 400g baby potatoes, halved
  • 2 sea bass fillets
  • 200g cherry tomatoes
  • 10 to 12 olives, halved
  • 1 small red onion, cut into wedges
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 lemon, juice only
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • Small handful parsley or basil, chopped
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220C or 200C fan.
  2. Toss the potatoes with 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper, and half the oregano. Roast for 20 minutes.
  3. Add the cherry tomatoes, olives, and red onion to the tray. Toss lightly and roast for 8 minutes.
  4. Season the sea bass, place it on top of the vegetables, and drizzle with the remaining oil and lemon juice.
  5. Roast for 8 to 10 minutes until the fish is just cooked and the tomatoes have slumped.
  6. Finish with the remaining oregano and the chopped herbs.
