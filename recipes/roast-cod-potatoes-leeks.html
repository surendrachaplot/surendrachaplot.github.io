Roast Cod with Potatoes, Leeks, and Mustard-Caper Dressing

40 min · Serves 2

This is the roast potatoes and fish version, but without turning dinner into a beige support group. The potatoes get properly crisp, the leeks go soft and sweet, and the mustard-caper dressing keeps the whole thing awake.

Ingredients

  • 400g baby potatoes, halved
  • 2 cod fillets, about 140 to 160g each
  • 1 large leek, washed well and sliced into thick rounds
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp capers, roughly chopped
  • 1/2 lemon, juice only
  • 1 small handful parsley, chopped
  • 1 garlic clove, grated
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220C or 200C fan.
  2. Toss the potatoes with 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, and pepper. Roast cut-side down for 20 minutes.
  3. Turn the potatoes, add the leek rounds, and toss everything with a little more oil if needed. Roast for 10 minutes more.
  4. Rub the cod with the remaining olive oil, season well, and lay it on top of the potatoes and leeks. Roast for 8 to 10 minutes until the fish flakes easily.
  5. Whisk together the mustard, capers, lemon juice, parsley, garlic, and 1 tbsp water to make a loose dressing.
  6. Plate the cod with the roast potatoes and leeks, then spoon over the dressing.
