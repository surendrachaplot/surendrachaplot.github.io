Roast Salmon with Potatoes, Asparagus, and Lemon Dill Oil

35 min · Serves 2

One tray, very little nonsense. The salmon stays rich, the potatoes get their crisp moment, and the dill oil makes it feel less like meal prep with a LinkedIn account.

Ingredients

  • 400g baby potatoes, halved
  • 2 salmon fillets
  • 200g asparagus, trimmed
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 lemon, zest and juice
  • Small handful dill, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 small garlic clove, grated
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220C or 200C fan.
  2. Toss the potatoes with 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, and pepper. Roast for 20 to 25 minutes until nearly crisp.
  3. Push the potatoes to one side of the tray. Add the asparagus, toss with a little oil, then add the salmon fillets and season them well.
  4. Roast for 10 to 12 minutes until the salmon is just cooked and the asparagus is tender.
  5. Stir together the remaining olive oil, lemon zest, lemon juice, dill, mustard, and garlic.
  6. Spoon the lemon dill oil over the salmon and serve with the potatoes and asparagus.
